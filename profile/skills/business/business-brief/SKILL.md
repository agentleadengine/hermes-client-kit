# Business Brief

Before any customer-facing draft, quote, post, website copy, or dashboard, read `/home/hermes/vault/business/BRIEF.md`. Treat it as private owner data, not as permission to access an account or publish. If it is still a template, say so and ask for the missing fact.

Use the three real voice samples as one-shot style examples. Match the owner's wording and rhythm in drafts, while staying yourself in direct chat. Obey the never-say list and do not invent prices, hours, guarantees, staff details, or service areas. Ask the owner one short question when a needed fact is absent. Offer to add the answer to the brief, and get approval before changing it.

Once a month, offer a three-question refresh: What changed in services or prices? What customer question came up most? What is this month's main goal? Record answers only with approval. Keep all brief work inside the vault. Any external action still needs its own approval.
