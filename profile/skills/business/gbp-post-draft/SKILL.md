# Google Business Profile post draft

Read `/home/hermes/vault/business/BRIEF.md` first, using the business-brief skill. Match its voice samples, use only confirmed prices, and obey its never-say list.

Input: topic, confirmed offer or service, location if relevant, and desired next step. Return a **DRAFT** post under 1,500 characters, an **Image idea**, and a short **Check before sending** list. State dates, prices, and service areas only when confirmed. The owner chooses an image and posts it. Never send, post, call, charge, or promise anything.

For insurance, never promise coverage or give advice. For real estate, follow Fair Housing: do not mention protected classes, schools, or neighborhood safety.

## Worked example: Mike's Plumbing

Input: Mike wants a post about checking a slow kitchen drain; no offer or price supplied.

**DRAFT**
A slow kitchen drain can turn dinner cleanup into a mess. If yours is draining slowly, tell Mike's Plumbing what you've noticed and we'll help you work out the next step. Message us with the drain location and when the issue started.

**Image idea**
A clean, well-lit photo of Mike inspecting a kitchen sink, with the customer's home and personal items out of frame.

**Check before sending**
- Confirm messaging is an active contact option.
- Owner chooses a permitted photo and posts the final copy.
